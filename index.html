How long does the institutional onboarding process take? ▼

Our standard corporate onboarding cycle takes 2-4 business days. This includes entity checks, regulatory compliance review, and sandbox access to setup payment API links.

Apex Corporate accounts do not carry rigid transactional ceilings. Transactions exceeding $15,000,000 USD only require primary compliance approval desk verification through the Client Portal dashboard.

Yes. We offer pre-built developer webhook frameworks and REST APIs supporting SAP, Oracle NetSuite, Microsoft Dynamics, and custom software systems.
